## Reason · 为什么改

The creator found v009 too much like an animated slide deck. A blinking still image plus UI transitions does not meet the animation goal. Keep the original cats and dry humor; prove continuous character action before making more episodes / 创作者指出 v009 像动态 PPT；静态猫加眨眼和转场不算达标。保留原猫和冷幽默，先验证连续表演，不靠扩集或加特效。

## Boundaries · 尚未解决

- This is articulated cutout animation, not full hand-drawn animation. Rigid limb pieces still have a limited range / 仍是关节分层动画，不是完整逐帧；刚性肢体活动范围有限。
- The later sheepish/proud/yawn/sleep changes still use discrete drawings; no claim of continuous turning, walking or lying down / 后半疑惑得意与哈欠睡姿仍切图，未做到连续转身走路躺下。
- No phoneme lip sync, new audio or new raster drawings were added / 未新增音素口型、音轨或图片。
- The GO key and task panel are story illustrations, not a real product recording / 按键与任务板是剧情示意，不是真实产品录屏。
- Next drawing work should supply matching intermediate reach/turn/crouch frames and joint underpainting, not unrelated pose PNGs / 下一批画稿应补同造型的伸爪转身下蹲过渡与关节底画，不再堆无关姿势。
- First review muted movement at phone size, then child speech timing. Actual browser recording and creator approval remain pending / 先手机尺寸静音验动作，再听童声节奏；浏览器实录及创作者确认仍待完成。
