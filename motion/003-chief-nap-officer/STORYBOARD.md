## Comic Engine · 笑点机制

Lucky sincerely thinks delegation makes his new title legitimate. Mochi expects a real job description; he supplies one with complete confidence, then demonstrates it by falling asleep. The contrast is visible: the team keeps working, the founder does not / Lucky 认真认为安排好工作就可以担任新职位；Mochi 等他解释职责，他理直气壮报出职位并马上示范午睡。团队仍在干活，老板已经睡着，反差由画面完成。

Do not frame Lucky as stupid or claim the tasks are completed. The product establishes the situation, not the final joke / 不把 Lucky 写成笨蛋，不宣称任务完成；产品建立情境，角色完成笑点。

## Screenplay · 英文成品脚本

```text
LUCKY: I started a company.
MOCHI: You have no employees.
LUCKY: About that.
[Lucky presses a key. Three illustrated tasks work in parallel.]
MOCHI: Then what do you do?
[Lucky holds for a beat.]
LUCKY: Chief Nap Officer.
[A short pause. Lucky yawns, lowers himself and curls up asleep.]
[Mochi watches. The tasks continue. She finally smiles.]
END CARD: Business as usual.
```
