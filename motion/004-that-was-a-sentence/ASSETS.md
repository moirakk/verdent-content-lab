## Image Sources · 图片来源

All character PNGs retain original alpha and bytes. Shared files are in [the pose library](../../characters/poses/README.md). Five added source frames and their exact prompts are recorded in [Animation prompts](../../characters/poses/PROMPTS-ANIMATION-V001.md). Built-in Image Generation was used; no raster image edits were made outside that tool / 所有角色 PNG 保留原始透明通道与字节；五张新增画稿和提示词见共享库。采用内置图像生成，不在工具外修改位图。

HTML/SVG uses paired eye drawings for blinks, separate clipped head/body layers, short pose transitions, timed reactions, head gestures, anticipation/recoil, camera reframing, folded paper and an earned final hold. This is painted **limited animation**, not frame-by-frame hand-drawn lip sync. Body/head clipping is runtime composition, not an alteration of source artwork / 通过眼睛画稿切换、头身分层、短姿势衔接、反应停顿、准备回弹、镜头和折纸构成水粉有限动画；不宣称逐帧手绘或音素口型。分层只发生在运行时，不改原素材。

The official logo is copied unchanged from [the supplied original](../../brand/assets/verdent-logo-original.png); multiply blending only removes the visual white rectangle during composition / 官方 Logo 使用原文件，只在合成显示时正片叠底，不重画、不拉伸。
