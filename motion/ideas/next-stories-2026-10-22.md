## 1. Office Upgrade · 办公室升级（已改编为 001 v004）

Desire / 欲望：Lucky proudly upgrades their workspace; both cats prefer a cardboard box to any actual office. / Lucky 得意升级办公室，两只猫却都更喜欢纸箱。

| Beat / 镜头 | English dialogue / 英文对白 | Acting / 动作 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Lucky: I upgraded the office. | He proudly pats a small cardboard box / 得意拍拍小纸箱 |
| 3–5s | Mochi: Where? | She surveys the whole room / 环顾整个房间 |
| 5–8s | Lucky: Corner office. | He slips into the box, pressing his cheek against its corner / 钻入纸箱、脸贴箱角 |
| 8–12s | Mochi: Room for two? | After a dry pause she steps closer / 停半拍后靠近 |
| 12–15s | Lucky: Open plan. | Both squeeze in; one flap pops open / 两只挤入、箱盖弹开 |

Visual payoff / 画面笑点：the “office upgrade” is a slightly squashed box, and Mochi joins instead of merely judging. / 升级只是压扁一点的纸箱，Mochi 也主动加入，不只是吐槽。

Source direction / 素材方向：use `scenes/box-living-room/keyframe-v001.png` as a setting reference, then make separate complete-cat and box-flap assets for animation; do not move a flat scene PNG and call it character acting. / 纸箱客厅可作场景参考，制作时需整猫与箱盖分层，不以整图移动冒充表演。

Production needs / 制作需求：whole-cat step-in/crouch sequence, physically grounded box contact, hinge-driven flap, dry plucked score; no forced product appearance. / 整猫进箱下蹲、落地接触、箱盖铰链动作、克制拨弦，不硬插产品。

## 2. The Meeting · 开会

Lucky: “Everyone agreed.”

Mochi: “Who was there?”

Lucky: “Me.”

Mochi looks at his reflection.

Lucky: “Unanimous.”

A tiny mirror is Lucky’s very agreeable meeting partner. Mochi silently turns it toward him after his claim. / 小镜子是全票通过的会议伙伴，Mochi 听完后默默把镜子转向他。

Needs / 需求：correct reflected screen direction, paw-mirror contact, a held eye reaction. / 镜像方向准确、爪与镜框接触、眼神停顿。

## 3. Reserved · 已预订

Lucky: “I saved you a seat.”

Mochi sees an empty chair.

Lucky: “The warm one.”

The actual seat is a tiny patch of sunshine on the floor.

Mochi quietly squeezes in beside him.

Warm character ending rather than another work/nap punchline. / 温暖角色收尾，不重复工作午睡梗。

Needs / 需求：sun patch grounded to floor, two complete-cat sit/settle sequences, independent eye contact. / 阳光落在地面、两套整猫坐下落稳、独立目光回应。
