# Visual Style · 视觉与声音规范

**The same original painted cats, acting through different life situations / 同一套原创水粉小猫，在不同生活情境里表演。**

## Fixed identity · 固定造型

Mochi: fluffy orange-gold, cream muzzle/chest, pink nose. Lucky: warm charcoal black, large ears, yellow-green eyes. Natural kitten proportions; broad broken matte oil/gouache masses. No fine-fur mascot redesign, glossy 3D cats or vector substitute / 橘猫与黑猫保持人设外貌，自然幼猫比例，粗而断续的哑光笔触，不换精细毛发吉祥物、亮面三维或矢量造型。

[Painting anchors](../characters/style-anchors/README.md) govern new poses. Keep the character recognisable at comparable camera angle and scale / 同角度同尺度对照参考，保持角色可辨认。

## Staging and story · 空间与剧情

Different rooms, seasons and props are welcome. Consistency concerns character/brush language, not repetition of one room. Keep cats prominent, feet grounded, props within reach and clear near/far occlusion / 场景季节道具可以变化，统一角色笔触，不重复同一房间；猫主导、脚落地、道具可触、遮挡远近清楚。

Current ordinary-life films use 4:5; Halloween uses 9:16. Leave readable safe margins. Prefer a visible opening event, a motivated response, one quiet reaction and a visual payoff / 日常片当前为 4:5，节日竖屏；留安全边距，以开场事件、反应、停顿和视觉反转组织故事。

Use head-anchored dialogue for new scenes; do not overlay persistent headings that dominate the characters. 003 retains its existing benchmark presentation until deliberately revised / 新场景使用头顶对白，不让常驻标题压过角色；003 保留现有基准形式，按明确需求再改。

## Acting · 表演

Anticipation → action/contact → reaction → settle. Use full connected redraws, eyes, ears, head angle and posture. No random rocking, constant bouncing, face crossfade ghosts or detached limbs / 动作有准备、接触、反应、落稳，用整猫、眼耳头姿，不随机摇晃弹跳、不重影换脸或拆出断肢。

Never cut paws/neck from flattened art and pivot without proper underpainting. Rejected 003 v010 detached a paw; keep that failed archive but do not reuse its rig / 不从扁平图硬拆爪颈旋转；003 v010 断爪失败保留，结构不复用。

Limited painted key-pose animation is the current implementation. Do not call it full frame-by-frame animation or phoneme lip sync / 当前为有限关键姿势动画，不宣称全逐帧或音素口型。

## Halloween clarification · 万圣节澄清

Preserve the painted room, white ghost-sheet Lucky and witch-hat Mochi. “More 3D” means solid laptop thickness/perspective/hinge/key contact and candy depth, not replacing the cats or room / 保留油画房间、白布幽灵和女巫帽，立体化仅针对电脑厚度透视铰链按键及糖果空间。

## Sound and controls · 声音控制

Childlike English speech: calm Mochi, eager Lucky. Use distinct original/licensed music and motivated action sounds; audible between lines, ducked during speech. No adult narrator pitched up, laugh track or identical score pasted over every scene / 英文童声姐姐稳弟弟活泼，独立配乐与动作音，空拍可听对白避让，不用成人升调、罐头笑声或每集同一音乐。

New story players wait for explicit Play with sound and keep pause/replay/seek/mute. The library unmutes and restarts the selected video and pauses other videos. Captions must still explain the story when muted / 新剧情显式点击带声，保留暂停重播进度静音；目录开当前片声音并暂停其他片；静音也要看懂。

## Review · 验看

Check recognisable identities, connected anatomy, head-bubble clearance, plausible contact/grounding, phone readability, actual audio presence and a held ending in the real browser. A Git sync or passing check is not film approval / 实测浏览器看造型结构、头顶间距、接触落地、手机可读、真实声音与结尾停顿，检查和同步不等于成片获批。
