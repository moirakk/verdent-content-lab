# Project Brief · 项目总纲

## Purpose · 目标

An original English-language painted comedy series about Mochi and Lucky living and building things together. Viewers should care about their relationship and the visual joke before noticing a product / 原创英文水粉小猫喜剧，先让观众喜欢角色关系和画面笑点，再自然注意到产品。

**Character first. Joke second. Product third. / 先人物，再笑点，最后产品。**

## Confirmed direction · 已确认方向

| Area / 项目 | Direction / 方向 |
| --- | --- |
| Mochi | Orange-gold fluffy older sister, cream muzzle/chest, pink nose; calm, rational, dry humor / 橘金蓬松姐姐、浅口鼻胸毛、粉鼻，冷静理性冷幽默 |
| Lucky | Warm charcoal black younger brother, large ears, yellow-green eyes; eager and curious / 温黑弟弟、大耳黄绿眼，热情好奇，不是笨蛋 |
| Painting | Broad broken matte oil/gouache brush masses; natural kitten proportions / 粗而断续的哑光油画水粉笔触，自然幼猫比例 |
| Acting | Whole connected cats, motivated anticipation/contact/reaction/settle; no detached cut-apart paws / 整猫动作有准备接触反应落稳，不硬拆出断爪 |
| Story | Short visual setup, character disagreement, held reaction, concise payoff; both cats have agency / 短视觉铺垫、人物分歧、停顿反应与简短反转，两只都有主见 |
| Variety | Different life settings and actions; avoid repeating one room or dialogue-only slides / 不同生活布景和动作，避免重复同一房间或对白幻灯片 |
| Speech | Childlike English voices, calm Mochi and lively Lucky; no adult narrator pitched up / 英文童声，姐姐稳弟弟活泼，不把成人旁白升调替代 |
| Music | Distinct original/licensed BGM and motivated SFX, audible between lines and quieter under speech / 独立原创或获许可配乐和动作音，空拍听得见、对白时避让 |
| Dialogue | Finished text English only, names Mochi/Lucky; use head-following bubbles where the scene supports them / 成品英文，直接叫名字，当前新剧情用头顶气泡 |
| Brand | Exact supplied logo, native aspect; product appearance is optional / 原 Logo 原比例，产品可不出现 |

The [blue studio](characters/style-anchors/blue-studio-keyframe-v001.png) and [Halloween reference](characters/style-anchors/halloween-approved-painting-2026-10-22.png) anchor painting style. The creator said 003 and 005 were acceptable benchmarks, then requested stronger variety, stories and BGM in other films. This is not final release approval / 两个参考锚定画风；创作者认为 003、005 还行，并要求其余片加强布景、剧情和配乐，不等于成片批准。

For Halloween, “more 3D” applies to laptop thickness/perspective, keys, hinge response and candy depth. Preserve painted cats, painted room, ghost-sheet Lucky and witch-hat Mochi / 万圣节立体感只用于电脑厚度透视、按键铰链和糖果远近，保留油画猫、房间与装扮。

## Current work · 当前作品

Actual selections, sound and exports live in [EPISODES.md](EPISODES.md) and [CONTENT_MAP.md](CONTENT_MAP.md). 001 is box-office comedy, 002 coffee escalation, 004 pre-opening sample testing; 003 and 005 retain their existing benchmarks / 实际选用见总览；新三集为纸箱办公室、咖啡升级、开业前试吃，003、005 保留基准。

Current films are limited-animation review drafts: complete painted key poses plus continuous props/camera, not fully hand-drawn in-betweens or phoneme lip sync / 当前为有限动画待审稿，完整关键姿势配连续道具镜头，不宣称完整逐帧手绘或音素口型。

## Product and publication · 产品与发布

Use one verified capability only when a story needs it, and label illustrative UI. [Product research](brand/VERDENT_FEATURE_MAP.md) and [strategy](brand/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md) support editorial work; proposed worlds and queues are not approved canon or schedules / 剧情需要时使用经核实的能力，区分示意界面；资料和策略不自动成为固定世界观或排期。

All motion films remain unpublished. Comic EP.05 has creator-confirmed publication but missing release details. Check [actual status](CONTENT_MAP.md) before making claims. Keep the repository private / 动态均未发布；漫画 EP.05 已由创作者确认发布，详情待归档；仓库保持私有。
