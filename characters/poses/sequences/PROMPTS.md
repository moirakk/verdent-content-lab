## Actual Output vs Intent · 实际结果与提示意图区分

Generated sheets do not perfectly satisfy requested uniform registration. We measured cell/foot coordinates and compensate only whole-character placement at runtime; source pixels stay unchanged. The v001 drafts' framing was rejected. v002's complete silhouettes and full action were reviewed, but final style/animation approval still belongs to the creator / 生成稿未完美满足统一定位，实际坐标另行实测，播放时只平移完整角色，不改源像素；初稿边缘不合格不用。修正版已查整猫轮廓与动作，最终画风表演仍待创作者确认。
