# Mochi

黄猫姐姐。冷静、理性、dry humor。它的淡定是一种节奏，不是对 Lucky 的嫌弃。

Yellow older sister; calm, rational, dry humor. Her composure sets the rhythm rather than belittling Lucky. Use short, precise, understated English lines. She likes coffee, clear plans, and quietly finishing things; she can also overthink, procrastinate, and secretly enjoy Lucky's ideas. She helps him without becoming a lecturing parent.

Appearance follows the orange cat at the back left of the reference photo: fluffy golden-orange fur, light tabby marks, cream muzzle and chest, pink nose, round eyes, triangular ears. Half-lidded eyes are an acting choice, not a fixed photographic feature. Use slow blinks, gentle breathing, and small tail-tip movement. Poses include staring, side-eye, typing, drinking coffee, and sleeping.

- 真猫参考：[照片与外貌说明](references/README.md)，照片左后方橘黄色猫。
- 外形锚点：橘金色蓬松毛、浅虎斑、浅奶油色口鼻与胸毛、小粉鼻、圆眼和三角耳。插画使用低饱和赭黄与原创简化轮廓；半垂眼睑用于表现冷静，不代表真猫固定眼型。
- 声线：短、平静、准确，最后半拍才给笑点。英文直接叫 Lucky。
- 喜欢：热咖啡、能说明白的计划、安静做完一件事。
- 缺点：过度理性，有时偷偷拖延；喜欢 Lucky 的点子但不轻易承认。
- 关系：可以吐槽 Lucky，也会帮它收尾；不是训话的家长。
- 初始姿势库：staring / side-eye / typing / drinking-coffee / sleeping。
- 动作幅度：慢眨眼、轻微呼吸、尾尖摆动；不频繁大幅挥爪。

对白样本：“Of course you do.” / “Define ‘quick’.” / “Let's start with one.”
